Projects

A few of the projects I am currently working on, mostly built around the codebases described on the Software page. This page will be updated as they develop.

Testing dark matter across models

Bringing DORCHA's zoom simulations together with Ashvini's semi-analytic model to compare galaxy populations across different dark matter models on a like-for-like basis — the same galaxy-formation model throughout, with only the dark matter physics varied. The central question is whether small-scale structure and galaxy properties genuinely retain information about the underlying dark matter model, and how reliably that information survives the modelling choices in between.

A Milky Way dark matter annihilation signal

Combining DORCHA's simulated Milky Way analogue haloes with survey and instrument forecasting, for facilities such as CTA and SKA, to work out how confidently a candidate dark matter annihilation signal could actually be detected and attributed, given realistic uncertainty in a halo's structure.

Dark matter and disc stability

Work on how different dark matter models affect the stability of galactic discs, including the roles of halo structure and baryonic contraction.

Dwarf galaxy simulations

Hydrodynamical simulations of isolated dwarf galaxies, built on the AGORA framework with a modified version of Arepo, used to calibrate the feedback physics that feeds into the semi-analytic and zoom-simulation work above.

DarkMaps

A developing programme concerned with dark matter, simulations, data, and the infrastructure required to connect them. It has several strands rather than a single fixed scope, and this page will be updated as they take shape.

Simulation and HPC/GPU methods

Ongoing work on cosmological simulation codes and the computational methods behind them, including GPU-enabled computing and the infrastructure needed to run and manage large simulation campaigns.
