Software

I write and maintain software as part of my research, most of it released openly. Most of it fits together: DORCHA produces the haloes, Ashvini models the galaxies that form within them, and imbas renders the results.

foraois

An open-source Python implementation for modelling populations of high-mass X-ray binaries.

github.com/doctorcbpower/foraois

Ashvini

A semi-analytic galaxy formation model built around Monte Carlo merger trees. I use it to test how choices made in modelling galaxy formation interact with the underlying dark matter physics.

github.com/doctorcbpower/Ashvini

DORCHA

A suite of cosmological zoom simulations of Milky Way analogue haloes, together with the orbit and initial-conditions tooling that supports it.

github.com/doctorcbpower/Dorcha

realta

A modernised Monte Carlo model for stellar and binary populations, extending the framework described in Power et al. (2009).

github.com/doctorcbpower/realta

imbas

A rendering engine that turns simulation snapshots — Gadget, SWIFT, Arepo — into publication-quality images.

github.com/doctorcbpower/imbas_renderer

A number of smaller scripts and utilities — for processing simulation output, computing power spectra, generating turbulent velocity fields, and setting up zoom initial conditions — are also on GitHub.
