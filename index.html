Chris Power

Cosmologist and computational astrophysicist, ICRAR / The University of Western Australia.

I use cosmological simulations to investigate how dark matter shapes the formation and structure of galaxies. Much of this work sits at the boundary between fundamental physics and observation: what a galaxy's structure can actually tell us about the dark matter it sits within, and how much of that inference survives the assumptions built into the simulations along the way.

I am also interested in the computational side of the work — the codes, infrastructure and numerical methods that make these calculations possible — and I try to build things that other researchers can use.

This site collects what I work on, the software I maintain, and a few ongoing projects. It is deliberately plain, and will grow as time allows.
