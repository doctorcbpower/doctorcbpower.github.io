Chris Power

Cosmologist and computational astrophysicist, ICRAR / The University of Western Australia.

I use cosmological simulations to test the nature of dark matter. Different dark matter theories — from cold dark matter through to models with self-interactions or wave-like behaviour — leave only subtle imprints on the structure of galaxies and the cosmic web, and those imprints are easily confused with the effects of ordinary galaxy-formation physics. Much of my work is about separating the two: holding galaxy formation fixed within a controlled simulation framework, so that a difference in outcome can be attributed to the dark matter physics rather than to a modelling choice.

I am also interested in the computational side of this work — the codes, infrastructure and numerical methods that make these calculations possible — and I try to build things that other researchers can use.

This site collects what I work on, the software I maintain, and a few ongoing projects. It is deliberately plain, and will grow as time allows.
