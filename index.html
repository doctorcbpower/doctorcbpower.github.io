Chris Power

Cosmologist and computational astrophysicist, ICRAR / The University of Western Australia.

My interest is in whether, and how reliably, the effects of dark matter physics can be extracted from cosmological simulations and observations. Different dark matter theories — from cold dark matter through to models with self-interactions or wave-like behaviour — leave only subtle imprints on the structure of galaxies and the cosmic web, and those imprints are easily confused with the effects of baryonic physics. Much of my work is about separating the two: holding the galaxy-formation model fixed gives a controlled comparison that isolates the effect of the dark matter model within that framework, though it does not remove every dependence on the modelling choices involved.

I am also interested in the computational side of this work — the codes, infrastructure and numerical methods that make these calculations possible — and I try to build things that other researchers can use.

This site collects what I work on, the software I maintain, and a few ongoing projects. It is deliberately plain, and will grow as time allows.
