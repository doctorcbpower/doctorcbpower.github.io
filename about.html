About

I am a cosmologist and computational astrophysicist at ICRAR, The University of Western Australia, based in Perth. I have an MA in theoretical physics from Trinity College Dublin (1999) and a PhD in computational cosmology from Durham University (2004). I held postdoctoral positions at Swinburne University of Technology and the University of Leicester before joining ICRAR/UWA in 2011, where I have since been an ARC Future Fellow, an Associate Professor, and, since 2021, a Research Professor.

I founded and built the ICRAR/UWA Cosmological Theory group, and have led theory efforts within the ARC Centres of Excellence for All-Sky Astrophysics (CAASTRO) and for All-Sky Astrophysics in 3 Dimensions (ASTRO 3D). Alongside my own research, I spend a fair amount of time on the computing infrastructure the field depends on: I sit on the National Computational Merit Allocation Committee, have chaired scientific HPC advisory committees for Astronomy Australia, and contributed to the Australian Academy of Science's Decadal Plan for Astronomy.

I work across most of what this kind of research requires: simulation codes, analysis pipelines, and the more traditional parts of the job — teaching, supervision and grant writing.

Supervision

I have supervised more than two dozen PhD and Masters students and around ten postdoctoral researchers since 2011. A number have gone on to faculty or postdoctoral positions elsewhere, in Australia and overseas, and others have moved into industry roles in data science and computing. If you are interested in working with me on dark matter, galaxy formation or computational astrophysics, feel free to get in touch.
